SitPred: a situation predictor for finance

SitPred is my own live app. You describe a situation with competing players and it predicts their moves using game theory. I designed the prompt logic, scoped the MVP, built it and shipped it on my own time.

The case study takes it into banking, with a deposit rate war as the first use case. The prototype brings the live app and the finance concept together: describe any situation to get the five-part prediction, or open the finance situation room and drag its assumptions to see what flips the call.

Shipped: a live app, plus a finance PRD, 8 personas, a real equilibrium engine and a prototype with decision memos.

AI agents · Trust and safety · iOS concept

Siri as an agent you can trust with money

What would it take for Siri to actually do things, like paying bills or moving meetings, without people worrying it will get it wrong? I worked through research, 8 personas, a competitive analysis and a PRD.

  • Siri shows a plan card before it acts, so you can approve, edit or cancel.
  • Every step gets a risk tier (T0 to T3). Payments need Face ID.
  • An activity log with undo, and scam payees blocked on the spot.

Apple ecosystem · Sync · Privacy by design

Live previews for iCloud Drive

iCloud Drive shows blank icons where Google Drive shows previews, so people can't tell their files apart. I traced the root cause and designed a fix that still works with Advanced Data Protection.

  • Previews are made on the device that uploads the file and encrypted with the file's own keys.
  • Quick Look streams a preview, so you don't have to download a 330 MB video on cellular.
  • Search inside scans, slides and screenshots, run on the device.
Shipped: 9 personas, a PRD in three phases (See it, Peek it, Find it) and a Mac, iPad and iPhone prototype with 5 persona tasks.
